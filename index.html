About Us

Leading Petroleum & Electrical Engineering Solutions

Petropump Repairer and Construction is a trusted engineering services provider specializing in comprehensive forecourt operations, fuel dispensing systems, and electrical infrastructure solutions. Established in 2016, we have developed a strong reputation for delivering reliable, safety-driven, and compliant services.

Our highly skilled team brings over nine years of combined experience, offering a full spectrum of services ranging from fuel system installations to complex maintenance operations and emergency repairs. Operating within one of the most strictly regulated industries, we adhere to the highest Safety, Health, Environmental (HSE) and SANS standards.
