Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often

What areas do you service?

We are based in Dawn Park, Boksburg, Gauteng, and primarily service the greater Johannesburg and Pretoria areas. For larger construction projects and ongoing maintenance contracts, we are able to travel to other provinces including the Free State. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency support?

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency breakdown response for fuel pump failures, electrical faults, and critical system issues. Our emergency line is always available to minimise your downtime and get your operations back online quickly.

Are your technicians certified and compliant?

Absolutely. Our senior technician is Trade Test qualified from Olifantsfontein and holds an N6 Electrical Engineering (Heavy Current) Diploma. All work adheres to SANS 10378, the OHS Act, and company HSE procedures. We are fully registered (Reg. No: 2025/178537/07) and COIDA certified (MAAA1669689).

What types of clients do you work with?

We serve retail fuel stations and forecourts, commercial fleet depots, logistics yards, industrial facilities, factories, mining operations, and other energy-dependent sites. Our solutions are tailored to fuel-intensive, mission-critical environments.

How quickly can you respond to a service request?

For emergency breakdowns, we aim to respond as quickly as possible — typically within a few hours depending on your location. For planned maintenance, installations, and construction projects, we schedule based on your operational requirements and our current pipeline.

Do you supply parts and components?

Yes. We source and supply only approved, durable components from reputable manufacturers. This includes fuel pump filtration systems, submersible pumps, motors, relays, solenoid valves, pulse generators, hoses, nozzles, and meters — ensuring longevity, performance, and reliability.
